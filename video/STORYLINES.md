# Spotter ad: three storylines

One thing to remember: **everything you do at the APU gym, from the door to your last set, lives in one app.**
Assumptions for all three: 30–40 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, no voiceover, a music bed plus UI sound effects (free, made locally),
real Spotter screens captured from the demo account (`/?demo=1`), every animation and transition hand-coded.

## A. "5:45 PM" (recommended: a real student moment anyone at APU knows)
- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day · one continuous take (the camera never cuts, it dives into the phone and back out).
- **What you see:** class ends, a student wonders if the gym is packed, and the phone answers every question on the way: crowd, clock-in, which machine, the sets, the PR, the meal after.
- **First 2 s:** a giant clock flips 17:44 → 17:45 on a lime field; the last digit drops and becomes the "Live crowd" number in the app: **55 / 60 · Packed**.
- **Beats:** class ends → "is it packed?" (live crowd, best time 19:30) → reveal: Spotter → scan QR, clocked in → 3D map flies to the squat rack → log sets, PR lands → feed likes pour in → ranks climb → nasi lemak macros → claim "Your whole gym, one app" → end card + Android download.
- **Look:** near-black ground with lime (#84cc16) as the only accent, Geist type, the phone frame as the one carrier the eye follows.
- **Sound:** a tight 110 bpm beat, a click on every tap, a rising whoosh into each dive, a hit on the PR.

## B. "Before Spotter"
- **Device / form:** life before and after · split screen to one.
- **What you see:** four panes of the old way churn at once (queue at the counter with your ID, circling the floor for a free rack, a scribbled notebook of lifts, guessing the calories in your lunch); then they collapse into one calm phone where each problem is solved in a tap.
- **First 2 s:** four panes snap in on the beat; a counter ticks "queue: 7 people" while a hand flips through a crumpled notebook.
- **Beats:** four old ways → collapse → reveal → each pane's answer in the app (QR, 3D map, workout log, cafeteria macros) → ranks payoff → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the old panes in desaturated grey paper tones, the new side in Spotter black and lime.
- **Sound:** a cluttered, off-beat loop that cuts to silence at the collapse, then a clean beat.

## C. "Heaviest Wins" (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument · cut on the beat.
- **What you see:** giant kinetic words and real numbers slam in on every beat ("SCAN." "LIFT." "EAT." "RANK.") and each word cracks open into the real screen that does it; pure energy, hype-reel style.
- **First 2 s:** "100 KG" fills the frame, the zero spins into a weight plate and rolls off-screen, revealing the Best Lifts card.
- **Beats:** number hook → SCAN → FIND → LIFT → EAT → RANK → the leaderboard payoff → claim → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed lime and black colour fields swapping every half beat, huge condensed type.
- **Sound:** a hard 128 bpm track, every cut on the kick.
